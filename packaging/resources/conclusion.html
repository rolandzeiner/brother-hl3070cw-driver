Updating? Printers that already use this driver have been updated and keep their settings. There is nothing more to do.

First installation: add the printer.

  1. Open System Settings > Printers & Scanners.
  2. If your printer is already in the list, select it and remove it.
  3. Click Add Printer, Scanner or Fax and choose your printer.
  4. In Use, choose Select Software and pick Brother HL-3070CW, native driver (open source), or for one of the other printers the entry with your model that ends in native driver (open source, untested). Then click Add.

If Brother HL-3070CW, native driver (open source) is not in the list, Brother's driver files were not found: install Brother's driver for the HL-3070CW, then run this installer again, or take the files from Brother's package with /Library/Printers/HL3070CW-Native/add-brother-data.sh (see the guide). Until then an HL-3070CW can use Brother HL-3070CW, PostScript (BR-Script 3).

Help and updates: github.com/rolandzeiner/brother-hl3070cw-driver
